## Final 7.2 result

The promoted policy is heuristic-first and uses two deterministic production
tapes. A public-opening classifier selects the anti route when the opponent has
four hands after the first turn and retains the general route otherwise. A
shared five-worker opening plus a step-1 inventory repair makes either route
mechanically valid. The classifier uses only public farm state, not player
names, hidden state, replay IDs, or seed fingerprints.

Compact CMA-ES searched only four new market-timing controls while keeping the
proven route and overlay values frozen. Its promoted values are a four-turn
lead, active on steps 48–669, capped at 20 units. The four-generation run used
10 candidates and 10 paired-seat games per candidate. Checkpoint:
`artifacts/tuning/cmaes-7.2-front-run.json`.
